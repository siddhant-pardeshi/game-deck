# Privacy

**The Game Deck collects nothing.**

- No accounts, no sign-ups, no email addresses.
- No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers of any kind.
- No network requests after the page loads — the games run entirely offline in your browser.

The only data stored is **on your own device**, in your browser's localStorage under the `sidgames.*` namespace:

- game progress (quests, unlocks, saves)
- personal best scores
- your settings (volumes, quality, reduced-motion, mascot visibility)

That data never leaves your device. To delete everything, use **Settings → Reset all progress** in any game, or clear the site's data in your browser.
